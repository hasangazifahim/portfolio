Mental Health Counseling Practice: AEO GEO & SEO Campaign

atlcounseling.com • Industries: Mental Health & Counseling +1 • Project Duration: 6 Months
Client Baseline & Goal Starting Metrics

The client started with 154 clicks and 3.85K impressions over the measured period. The goal was to improve local search visibility for an Atlanta-based counseling practice.

What I Did:

  • Conducted AEO GEO & SEO audit and local competitor analysis to identify visibility gaps against other Atlanta-area counseling practices.
  • Ran keyword research targeting high-intent service terms (individual counseling, couples counseling, child/teen therapy, CBT, DBT, executive function coaching, trauma, EMDR, anxiety, parenting stress, divorce support).
  • Optimized on-page for core service pages (Adult Therapy, Child & Teen Counseling, Couples Counseling, Executive Function, Trauma & EMDR) to better align with search intent and answer-focused search needs.
  • Fixed technical SEO and Google Search Console issues affecting indexing.
  • Wrote and published blog content addressing real client search queries (anxiety treatment, CBT vs. DBT, medical vs. non-medical struggles) to build topical authority.
  • Built foundational backlinks (local citations, profile/directory listings) to strengthen local relevance for an Atlanta-based practice.

Mattress & Sleep E-Commerce Brand: AEO GEO & SEO Campaign

sleeplabpty.com • Industries: E-Commerce / Home & Furniture +1 • Project Duration: 3 Months
Client Baseline & Problem Starting Metrics

It’s a Shopify store selling Serta and Simmons mattresses, adjustable bases, and sleep accessories, pulling in just 70 clicks and 1.67K impressions over a three-month stretch. The brand needed its product and category pages to improve organic search visibility for Panama shoppers searching in both English and Spanish.

Where I Focused:

  • Keyword research across bilingual search terms (colchones Panamá, Serta vs. Simmons, adjustable bases) to catch both English and Spanish-language shoppers.
  • On-page SEO for the store's collection pages, including Mattresses, Serta, Simmons, Bases, and Accesorios, tightening titles, descriptions, and internal linking between categories.
  • Category page content writing for the Mattresses, Bases, and Accessories collections, since these were thin templated Shopify listings with little unique copy.
  • Technical SEO cleanup addressing Search Console indexing gaps across the product catalog.
  • Created blog content covering mattress guides and sleep tips to support broader organic search visibility.
  • Guest post placed on relevant home, wellness, & lifestyle sites to build authority for the domain.
  • Competitor analysis against other Panama-market mattress retailers to identify keyword and content gaps.

EXECUTION FRAMEWORK

SEO Process

A practical SEO process based on research, prioritization, implementation, and performance monitoring.

01

Understand

Understand the website, business goals, search intent, competitors, and technical environment.

02

Audit

Identify technical, content, on-page, local, and authority bottlenecks using SEO crawling and analysis tools.

03

Research

Analyze keywords, search intent, competitor content gaps, and relevant business and search opportunities.

04

Prioritize

Determine which priority SEO actions should be addressed first based on priority, potential impact, and implementation effort.

05

Implement

Implement or recommend technical fixes, schema markup, title tags, content improvements, and redirects where needed.

06

Monitor

Track Search Console performance, impressions, clicks, rankings, and new organic queries.
